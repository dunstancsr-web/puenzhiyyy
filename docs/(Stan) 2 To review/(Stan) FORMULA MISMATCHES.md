## days_of_cover

Spec (design.md, "Days / Months of Cover"): available_qty / avg_daily_30d (Not Applicable when zero)

Code: available_qty / blended_daily_usage, rounded to whole days, where blended_daily_usage = half the 30 day average plus half the 90 day average. The same blended rate drives the projection, ABC and compliance, which the spec already documents as blended.

What changes on screen if the spec is applied: applying the spec's 30 day rate changes no alert or health status on the 15 Sep seed, but every displayed days-of-cover figure moves (for example BM-5KG 292 to about 313), and cover would jump whenever one busy or quiet month passes.

Recommendation: keep the code, correct the spec (design.md and requirements.md REQ-05): one demand rate across the app, and a smoother figure for decisions.

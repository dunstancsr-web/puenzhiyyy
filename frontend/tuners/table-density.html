What the numbers mean. Rows shown is how many read in full before the fade. Fade height is how far up the gradient reaches from the bottom of the table, so it wants to be roughly one row tall: shorter and the fade stops mid-row, taller and it starts eating a row you meant to be readable. Button bottom is the gap under the floating button, and the one worth watching, since a button taller than the row it sits on will hang past the table's edge. The measurement panel on the left flags that as it happens.

What is this?
The share of your portfolio sitting in the sweet spot - not so low you risk running out, not so high you're wasting money holding it.
How to read it
Target is 80% or higher. Below that, too much of the portfolio is either running low or piled up above what's needed.

WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for body text, AAA 7:1. Both ends of the gradient are measured because the fill is not flat. Figures composite the fill over the backdrop's dominant colour and ignore blur, which in practice helps legibility by averaging busy detail into a smooth wash, so treat these as the conservative floor.

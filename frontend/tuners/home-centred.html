Centred, device-aware

Option B's single centre axis with option D's honesty about the device, and none of D's split columns. Three ways to separate what you can do here from what happens on the floor, all on one axis.

Two other changes are in every variant. The question moves up to 30px and the sub-line to 22px, which is the two steps up you asked for while keeping two steps between them: at 22px alone the sub-line would have matched the question exactly and flattened the pair. And the help is back, as an i in each card's corner that opens on hover or focus, the same behaviour HoverHint already has in the app, rather than three repeated "What is this?" buttons.

The simplest structure of the three, and the only one that needs no layout change at all on a phone: it is already one column.
A narrow centred column makes the rows short enough that the arrow stays near the label rather than stranded at the far edge of the screen.
Cost. Three stacked rows in a 580px column start to look like a settings list rather than a front door.
On the type change. Both sizes moved, not just one. The sub-line going two steps up on its own would have landed it at 22px, the same size as the question, and two lines of identical size do not read as a heading and a caption, they read as a paragraph. Moving the question to 30px at the same time keeps the gap. If the pair now feels too loud for the top of the page, the fallback is question 22 and sub-line 17, which is one step each and preserves the relationship.

Stock Position — 4 Visual Concepts

Each concept shows Available stock / Reorder Point / Gap in a single table column.
Pick the one that makes sense at a glance, or mix elements from multiple concepts.

Concept A
Zone Bar — "Fuel gauge"

The bar is divided into coloured zones: red (danger), orange (reorder zone), green (healthy), purple (overstock). A black tick mark shows the reorder point. The fill colour shows the zone you're currently in. Best for: managers who think visually and want to see the full range at once.

Concept B
Half-Gauge Dial — "Speedometer"

A half-circle gauge shows stock level from empty to max. The arc is colour-coded by zone. A needle marks the reorder point. The number in the centre shows available stock. Best for: high-impact at-a-glance reading, especially for RED/ORANGE items on a management screen.

Concept C
Stacked Pill — "What's in the tank, broken down"

A single wide pill shows the full stock composition: reserved orders (dark), safety stock buffer (orange), free available stock (green), and overstock (purple). Numbers annotate each segment. Best for: understanding why the available number looks the way it does.

Concept D
Delta Card — "The number that matters, front and centre"

A two-part compact card. Left side: available stock with a tiny bar showing position between reorder and max. Right side: the gap as a large bold signed number (+ or −). Colour does the rest. Best for: dense tables with 20+ SKUs where you need to scan fast. The signed gap number is the only thing you read.
